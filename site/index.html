AirDrop, meet Windows.

HowlDrop puts your PC on your iPhone's AirDrop share sheet. Photos, videos, links and files land in seconds, and go back to the phone just as easily. No Mac, no app on the iPhone, no cloud.

Download for Windows How to set it up

Needs a USB Wi-Fi stick. Which one?

Everything you'd expect from AirDrop

Your PC shows up on the share sheet under its own name. Tap it like any other device.

Fast

A 15 MB iPhone video lands in about 3 seconds, straight over Wi-Fi.

Both ways

Drag files onto the window, or right-click any file and choose Send to, iPhone.

Contacts, like an iPhone

Your contacts go straight through, anyone else waits for Accept. Or Contacts Only.

Right by the clock

A card for every transfer: progress, a thumbnail, Open and Show in folder.

Links and text too

Shared links open in your browser. Shared text is on your clipboard, ready to paste.

Phone to PC, nothing between

Peer to peer, as AirDrop is. No account, no upload, nothing to install on the iPhone.

It asks, it shows, it's done

Each AirDrop gets a card above the tray, the way Windows does notifications.

Set it up in a few minutes

Your PC's built-in Wi-Fi can't speak AirDrop's Wi-Fi on Windows, so HowlDrop brings its own: a USB stick, run by a small Linux that HowlDrop sets up and looks after for you. You just see a tray icon.

Windows 11 or 10with WSL 2
A USB Wi-Fi stickMediaTek MT7612U: Alfa AWUS036ACM, Netgear A6210 and others. The list
Bluetooth on the PCto wake the iPhone when sending to it
  1. Turn on WSLIn a terminal opened as administrator: wsl --install, then restart.
  2. Get HowlDropDownload howldrop.exe, put it somewhere it can stay (like %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\HowlDrop) and run it. Nothing to install. If SmartScreen stops it: More info, Run anyway.
  3. Plug in the stick and switch it onClick the owl by the clock, then the gear, and turn on AirDrop on this PC. The first time, HowlDrop fetches what it needs and Windows asks once to share the stick. When the dot turns green, you're on the share sheet.
  4. AirDrop somethingOn the iPhone: Share, AirDrop, your PC. To send to the phone, set its AirDrop to Everyone for 10 Minutes and drop files on the HowlDrop window.

Got Home Assistant? HowlDrop also runs as a Home Assistant add-on that catches AirDrops and forwards them to your PC over the network. HowlDrop is built on Owly, an open implementation of AirDrop.
